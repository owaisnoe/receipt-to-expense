# Deck v2: design patterns taken from Mobbin

1. **Product hero** ([Ramp](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/83a61ac1-4ad8-48d0-91be-13ac7b374e22), [Monarch](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/5e856b27-50dc-48c3-8378-05260573e2b8)).
   - Structure: a small eyebrow line, then a huge one-line headline, a short grey subtitle, and the real product shown in a large framed area.
   - Used on: the title slide, with the live app in a browser frame.
2. **Stat row, numbers only** ([Wise](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/7dfcd207-47ec-4cf2-9988-85371589abc4), [Revolut](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/b5d03099-8332-47e5-bd72-ed80d0d9756b)).
   - Structure: very large bold numbers with small labels, separated by thin vertical dividers, with no cards.
   - Used on: the title slide stats and the dark "numbers" slide (Revolut's dark variant).
3. **Numbered steps on thin rules** ([Beside](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/2cfd3169-f61f-4ca5-bd2a-67627febbc84), [Escape Cafe](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/68d54554-65f5-4fd2-a974-c30e7c9eaffc), [Titan](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/0d103afa-c85e-4568-a98a-0a1e7cbd5e8f)).
   - Structure: a mono-spaced "HOW IT WORKS" eyebrow, then 01 / 02 / 03 numerals with bold titles and grey copy under a hairline rule.
   - Used on: "How it works".
4. **Device mockup beside the copy** ([Aave](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/fb1684ae-9a5f-473d-9c3c-961aa6d0e46d), [Jeton](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/091299d9-899c-4a1b-9759-4892aadd2a99)).
   - Structure: a phone frame holding the real app screen.
   - Used on: the demo slide (browser frame plus phone frame plus QR).

**System:**
- **Grid:** 12 columns on a 13.33 in canvas with 0.75 in margins.
- **Colour:** white background, near-black text #0b0f19, grey #6b7280, a single emerald accent #0f9f6e, and 0.75 pt hairlines #e5e7eb.
- **Type:** Segoe UI.
- **Avoided:** pastel boxes, emoji, icon circles, rows of identical cards, and all-caps pills. Saam / Daam / Dand / Bhed appear only as letter-spaced grey kickers.
